5. Vimeo Videos

Our website embeds videos from Vimeo (Vimeo.com, Inc., 330 West 34th Street, New York, NY 10001, USA).

As soon as you open a page with embedded videos, a connection to Vimeo's servers is established automatically, and we also load the Vimeo player script from player.vimeo.com. As a result, Vimeo receives your IP address and technical details such as browser, device, operating system, the page you visited and the time of the request. This happens without any action on your part and regardless of whether you actively play a video. The videos run muted in background mode (autoplay) as soon as they come into view; no interaction is required.

We embed the videos with the "Do Not Track" parameter (dnt=1). According to Vimeo, this means no tracking cookies are set and no session data is collected for analytics purposes.

Vimeo.com, Inc. is based in the USA. The transfer of your data there relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR); Vimeo is certified under this framework.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in presenting our treatments in an engaging way. You may object to this processing at any time under Art. 21 GDPR (see Section 9 for contact details). You can also prevent the videos from loading by using a script or content blocker in your browser. Vimeo's privacy policy: vimeo.com/privacy
